How the Circle works

Three kinds of support, one goal

Group tutorials

Live sessions on the courses students find hardest, taught step by step, with room for every question.

Regular check-ins

Set your goals, report your progress, and stay accountable to a group aiming for the same result.

One-on-one guidance

Stuck on a topic or losing motivation? Message your mentor directly and get personal help.

Who it's for

Students who want the top grade and will work for it

  • 01Undergraduates in geology, geophysics, and related earth science courses.
  • 02Students at any university. The Circle runs online, so your school is not a barrier.
  • 03Anyone ready to show up for sessions, report honestly, and put in the study hours.
  • 04Final-year students who need guidance on projects and research writing.
Meet your mentor

Abubakar Sadiq Musa

First-class geology graduate (CGPA 4.79/5.00) and Best Graduating Student of his department, now a master's student in Exploration Geophysics at the Federal University of Lafia.

As President of the NMGS Student Chapter he led more than 350 student members, and the peer tutorial network he ran served over 300 students. In 2024 the chapter named him Best Tutor of the Year and Role Model of the Year.

This year, six geology graduates he mentored finished with first class. The Sadiqora Circle is how he is opening that mentorship to more students.
