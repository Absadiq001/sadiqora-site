Power skills

The human skills that decide how far you go

Communication

Say what you mean, clearly and briefly, in writing and in conversation, to lecturers, employers and teams.

Presentation and public speaking

Structure a talk, handle nerves, and hold a room, from project defences to interviews.

Emotional intelligence

Understand your own reactions and other people's, handle conflict and feedback, and work well with others.

Critical thinking

Question claims, weigh evidence, spot weak arguments, and make decisions you can explain.

Teamwork and leadership

Take responsibility, coordinate people, and lead a group project without taking over.

Time and self management

Plan your week, beat procrastination, build habits, and finish what you start.

Technical skills

The tools that multiply your skills

AI tools for study and work

Use AI to read faster, plan and draft better while staying the author. Prompting, checking outputs and knowing the limits.

Data and machine learning

From a dataset to a working model in Python, with real earth-science examples. No heavy maths needed to begin.
