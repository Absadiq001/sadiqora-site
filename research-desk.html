What we help with

Six ways the Research Desk can move your work forward

Data analysis and charts

Clean your dataset, choose the right statistics, and turn results into figures you can defend. Excel, Python and standard geoscience workflows.

GIS and mapping

Georeferencing, geological and sample-location maps, coordinate systems, and clean map layouts for your project or report.

Geophysical data processing

Magnetic, radiometric and gravity data: filtering, derivatives, interpretation and honest reading of what the maps do and do not show.

Proposal and design feedback

A clear research question, a method that can answer it, and a reviewer's eye on your proposal before your supervisor sees it.

Project and thesis coaching

Regular check-ins to plan chapters, keep to deadlines, and strengthen your argument. You write it; we help you write it better.

Presentations and posters

Review and coaching for defences, conference talks and posters, including practice sessions and sharper slides.

How it works

Three steps, no guesswork

  1. 1Tell us what you need. Send your topic, level, deadline and what you already have, by WhatsApp or email.
  2. 2Agree the scope. You get a plain reply on what is possible, how long it takes, and what it costs, before any work starts.
  3. 3Work together. We process, explain and review in stages, so you understand every result and can explain it to your supervisor.
Our one firm rule

We help you do your own work better. We do not write assessed work for you, sell finished projects or theses, or fabricate data. Your degree has to mean something, and so does ours.

Technical help such as processing a dataset, drawing a map or checking a method is always explained to you and credited as the help it is.

Who it is for

From final-year projects to small exploration teams

  • BSc students with a final-year project and a dataset they are unsure how to treat.
  • MSc and PhD researchers who need a second opinion on method and interpretation.
  • Early-career geoscientists and small teams who need data turned into a clear map or report.
